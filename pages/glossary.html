Glossary

An evolving glossary of core terms used in the context of Digital Trace.

Digital Trace
Every click, purchase, or location you leave online. Example: The app records how long you look at a post.Ambient Data Collection Data collected in the background, without you doing anything.Example: A weather app tracks your location all day.
Consent
Saying 'yes' to data collection, often without reallychoosing. Example: Clicking 'Accept All' just to read an article.
Point Cloud
How this work shows the Data Double: many single points that grow into one large mass. Example: Every click is one point.
Data Profile
The Data Double, used by companies and institutions to make decisions about you. Example: A rental application is rejected because of a low score.
Algorithmic Profiling
Sorting people into groups automatically, based on their data. Example: You and a friend see different prices for the same product.
Black Box
A system where you see the input and the result, but not what happens in between. Example: Your loan is rejected and nobody can explain why.
Surveillance Capitalism
A business model that uses human behavior as raw material for profit (term by Shoshana Zuboff). Example: The app is free because your data is the product.
