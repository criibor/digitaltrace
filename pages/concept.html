Concept

Every click, search and like leaves a trace. Together, these traces form a data double: a digital version of you that companies and algorithms know well, but that you never get to see. The video makes this double something you can feel. Cool, metallic point clouds grow slowly in an empty digital space, supported by sound. The website adds a second layer with background, process and a glossary of key terms. The idea is to first feel the topic, then understand it.The final piece is planned for an exhibition.
