Process

At the start was the question of how to translate an abstract, invisible phenomenon like the Data Double into something visual and acoustic.The point cloud emerged as the central design element: individual points in digital 3D space that condense into a growing, amorphous mass, not unlike how individual data traces combine into a profile. The technical execution took place in 3D, with a focus on the movement, density, and materiality of the point cloud.In parallel, a fitting sound was developed: rhythmic, layered with depth, metallic and experimental, with occasional softer tones woven in. Image and sound were tuned to each other so that together they create the cool, alien atmosphere the topic calls for.The process was shaped by repeated testing and refinement, until form, movement, and sound conveyed the intended feeling of intangibility and strangeness.
