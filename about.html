About

Digital Trace is about the data double: the digital version of you that is built from the data you leave online, and that you never get to see. A video with sound turns this hard-to-grasp feeling into a cool, metallic world of growing point clouds. The video stays on the emotional side, while the website makes the topic easier to understand, for example with a glossary of key terms. The work is planned for an exhibition.
